# Spotify India — User Engagement & Listening Time

## 📌 Project Overview

This project explores the business problem of low user engagement and listening time among Spotify users in India.

The objective was to identify potential factors affecting engagement, understand user pain points, define measurable business outcomes, and translate the findings into structured business requirements.

**Project Code:** BAF-13  
**Project Type:** Business Requirements Document (BRD)  
**Prepared by:** Shivika Agrawal  
**Date:** July 2026

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Spotify India faces challenges related to user engagement, listening-session duration, retention, and conversion from Free to Premium.

The project investigates how factors such as:

- Regional-language personalization
- Recommendation relevance
- Repetitive content
- Advertisement and skip-limit friction
- Content discovery
- Context-aware recommendations
- Network and usage conditions

may affect listening behaviour and engagement.

## 📊 Key Business Metrics

The project identified listening time as the central engagement metric and connected it to broader business outcomes such as:

- Ad impressions
- Free-to-Premium conversion
- Customer retention
- Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)
- Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)

The project used an assumed baseline of approximately **35 listening minutes per day** and defined target improvements for engagement, conversion and retention. These figures were treated as project assumptions to be validated through further analysis.

---

## 🧩 Key Findings

The research highlighted several potential engagement challenges:

1. **Regional relevance**
   - Regional-language listening represents an important opportunity in the Indian market.
   - Onboarding and recommendations may not sufficiently capture regional preferences.

2. **Recommendation fatigue**
   - Repetitive recommendations can reduce continued engagement.

3. **Free-tier friction**
   - Advertisement interruptions and skip limitations can contribute to early session exits.

4. **Content discovery**
   - Users may not discover the wider range of playlists and podcasts available on the platform.

5. **Contextual listening**
   - Listening behaviour varies across contexts such as commuting, studying and household activities.

6. **Competitive pressure**
   - Bundled and free alternatives create additional challenges for user retention and engagement.

---

## 💡 Product Outcomes

The project identified five broad areas for improving engagement:

- Keep recommendations feeling fresh
- Increase listening through social/shared experiences
- Expand regional-language content and personalization
- Carefully evaluate Free-plan limitations
- Build stronger daily listening habits

These outcomes were translated into business requirements rather than detailed technical implementation specifications.

---

## 📈 KPI Framework

The project developed a KPI framework around:

**Listening Time → Session Duration → Skip Rate → Retention → Premium Conversion → ARPU/LTV**

The framework was designed to provide Product, Analytics, Marketing and Engineering teams with a common way to evaluate engagement.
